* **Subtitle / short description (≤80 chars):** Your shifts, offers, leave and clockings in one place.

## Full description
Tempo shows you the roster your manager has published and lets you respond to it.

• See your upcoming shifts: times in your site's time zone, where you work, your break and any instructions
• Get told when your roster is published, changed or cancelled
• Accept or decline extra shifts your manager offers you
• Ask for leave and see whether it was approved
• Mark times you can't work
• Check your clockings and when your hours are approved
• Choose which notifications you get and when you're reminded before a shift

Notifications on your lock screen never show shift details; open the app for those.

Tempo is provided through your employer. You need an invitation from your manager to sign in.

**Shared tablets:** Tempo can also run on a company tablet as a clock-in kiosk. Your manager sets that up.
